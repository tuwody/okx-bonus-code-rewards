## Six clauses that decide whether you actually get paid

These live in the terms and FAQ rather than the marketing page, and each one has cost somebody a reward.

**The withdrawal wait runs up to 45 days.** A crypto reward can be traded the moment it lands, but it cannot leave the exchange until a qualifying period of up to 45 days ends. The release date is in Rewards → My Rewards.

**Deposits are measured net.** Qualifying withdrawals are subtracted from qualifying deposits. Sending funds to another OKX user or into OKX Pay also reduces that balance, which can drop you below a task threshold before verification completes.

**The hold moves with the market.** A reward paid in a volatile coin creates a balance hold equal to that quantity at its live price. Sell the reward, watch the coin rise, and the hold can exceed what you received, temporarily locking part of your own money. OKX's terms describe this as intended behaviour, not a bug.

**Four pair types never count.** USDC/EUR, USDT/USDC and USDC/USDG are excluded from trading tasks, as are zero mark-up conversions such as USDC/USD, along with zero-fee stablecoin pairs generally.

**The clock starts at registration, not at your first deposit.** You get 14 days from sign-up to finish referral-linked trading tasks, and each Welcome Rewards card carries its own separate deadline. Expired means forfeited.

**Rewards-funded trades do not count.** Trades paid for with rebate cards, contract vouchers, USDC fee waivers or welcome bonuses are excluded from deposit and trading tasks in most campaigns. Trading your bonus to unlock more bonus does not work.

## Referral code versus affiliate link: why "up to 50%" is not your bonus

Search results mix two different programmes, and the confusion is expensive.

|  | Referral program | Affiliate program |

| Who joins | Any verified main account, no application | Applicants approved by OKX after an audience review |
| What the inviter earns | Fixed or randomised crypto per invitee who completes tasks | Up to 50% of the invitee's trading fees, settled in USDT |

| Can the inviter pass on a fee share? | No adjustable share | Yes, the affiliate sets the percentage returned |
| What the invitee gets | Welcome Rewards enrolment | Welcome Rewards plus the affiliate's rebate |

The "50%" you see quoted is the ceiling on what an affiliate can earn from OKX. The 15% or 20% or 30% figures are what an affiliate chooses to pass back. A referral code from a friend gives you no rebate share at all unless that friend is running an affiliate arrangement.
